About Trejo DevCo

A one-person game studio in Texas.

Trejo DevCo LLC is a one-person indie game studio in Texas, run by Trey Crawford. Tee Town: Golf Course Builder is the studio's first game. It launched on November 4, 2026, on the App Store and Google Play.

The idea is simple: build a golf course you're proud of, watch golfers play it, then play it yourself and share it with everyone else. I design and build Tee Town myself, post updates on TikTok and Instagram, and read every support email.

What's coming next

  • Holiday Season, December 1 – January 1, with limited-time holiday items
  • Clubs: team up with other players and compete against other clubs
  • 1v1 matches with handicap-based matchmaking
  • Course of the Week, voted by the community
  • Terrain sculpting for even more control over your holes
  • More themed packs

Plans can change, but this is what's on the board right now. Follow @trejogolfs on TikTok and @teetowngolfgame on Instagram for updates.
