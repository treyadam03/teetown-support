Tee Town Simulator – Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · TrejoDevCo

Tee Town Simulator ("the app") is made by TrejoDevCo ("we"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the game works fully without an account, we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Playing without an account

If you choose "Play offline", your course and progress are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent to us, except that the app may ask our server for the current date so seasonal events start and end on time. That request contains no personal information.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional and unlock online features (cloud backup, sharing courses, ratings, leaderboards and tournaments). When you sign in we collect:

  • Sign-in details: your email address, or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple (or Google, if offered). If you use Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email. Apple may share your name the first time you sign in; we store it with your account but never show it to other players.
  • Your username: the name you choose, which other players can see. Until you pick one, you're shown as "Golfer" plus a short code, never your real name.
  • Game data: your saved courses, golfer level and skills, collection and daily-bonus progress (for cloud backup and moving to a new phone).
  • Things you share: courses you publish (with their names and descriptions), rounds and scores, ratings, and tournaments you host or enter. These are visible to other players.
  • Safety records: reports you file about other players' content, players you block, and when you agreed to the community rules.

We use this information only to run the game's features: to back up and restore your progress, to show shared courses, leaderboards and tournaments, and to keep the community safe (reviewing reports and removing content that breaks the rules).

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details; we only learn which items you unlocked.

Who processes your data

Online data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, which processes it on our behalf. Apple (and Google, if you use Google sign-in) handle sign-in. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Reports may be kept for a limited time after they're resolved so we can deal with repeat abuse.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Menu → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything online tied to it: cloud backups, shared courses with their ratings and leaderboards, scores, tournaments and your username. The game saved on your phone stays unless you delete the app.

Children

The app is a family-friendly game. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Players never see each other's real names or contact details. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access rules on our database let each player read and change only their own private data.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app.
