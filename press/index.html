Short description

Tee Town: Golf Course Builder is a free-to-play mobile game where you design a golf course hole by hole, watch golfers play it and react, then play every hole yourself. Share your course, play courses built by other players, enter tournaments and climb the World Ranking.

Long description

In Tee Town, you're the course designer, the owner and the club champion. Start with a patch of land and build a golf course hole by hole: shape the terrain, cut fairways and greens, dig bunkers, add water and plant trees.

Golfers start showing up as soon as you open. They play your course, react to every hole and pay green fees, so a course they love earns more. Hit goals to raise your course level and grow from a small municipal course to an 18-hole resort with a driving range, golf inn, helipad and Grand Clubhouse.

Then grab your clubs. You can play every hole yourself: pick a club, aim, shape a draw or fade and add spin. Each round earns XP to level up your golfer's power, accuracy, fade, draw, spin, recovery and putting.

Share your course for other players to play and rate, explore courses built by the community and chase their records. Enter solo tournaments or hosted tournaments against real players with real in-game purses, win trophies for your trophy case and climb the World Ranking. Optional themed packs, like Garden Party, Alpine Lodge and Desert Oasis, add new ways to make a course your own.

Tee Town is designed, built and run by Trejo DevCo, a one-person studio in Texas.
