Creator Program

Make Tee Town videos? Earn when your fans support you.

5%

of every purchase made by players who support your code.

@you

An @ tag next to your name and your course, so other players know it's you.

Tier 2

Your own visitor model: a golfer based on you who visits courses around Tee Town.

How it works

  1. Apply by email. Tell us where you post and send a video you've made.
  2. Get your code. We set up your creator code and a dashboard where you can see your totals.
  3. Share it. Players enter your code once. At every purchase they see a "Supporting @you" line, already ticked, that they can change with one tap. Supporting you doesn't change what they pay.
  4. Get paid. You earn 5% of each supported purchase, paid quarterly once you've earned at least $25.

Tiers

Tier 1: Creator

  • Your own creator code
  • 5% of supported purchases, paid quarterly ($25 minimum)
  • An @ tag shown next to your name and your course
  • A dashboard with how many purchases and players used your code

Tier 2: Featured creator

  • Everything in Tier 1
  • Your own visitor model in the game: a golfer based on you who shows up on courses around Tee Town

For creators we work with closely. Ask about it when you apply.

Players' privacy

Creators see totals only: how many purchases and players used their code, never who those players are.
