Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tap "Play offline" and the whole game works on your phone. An account (email, or Sign in with Apple on iPhone) adds cloud backup, sharing your course, leaderboards and tournaments.

How do I move my course to a new phone?

Sign in on your old phone first; your course and golfer back up automatically every couple of minutes (or use Menu → Account → Back up now). Then sign in with the same account on the new phone.

I bought something in the Pro Shop but it's missing.

Open the Pro Shop and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If it still doesn't show up, email us.

How do I report or block another player?

Tap the flag on a shared course or tournament, or long-press a name on a leaderboard. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Blocked players' courses, names and scores are hidden from you; manage them in Menu → Account → Blocked players.

When do seasonal events happen?

Events run on set dates (Spooky Season is all of October). Event items can only be picked up while the event runs, but anything you got stays yours afterward.

Delete your Tee Town account

In the app: Menu → Account → Delete account, then confirm.

Without the app: email support@trejodevco.com with the subject "Delete my Tee Town account" and your in-game username.
