How it works

Three steps, and then you do them all again with a bigger budget.

  1. 1

    Build

    Lay out fairways, greens, bunkers, water and trees, then add the buildings golfers love.

  2. 2

    Watch

    Golfers play your course, react to every hole and pay green fees. Happy golfers mean a bigger budget.

  3. 3

    Play

    Tee it up yourself. Aim, shape a draw or fade, add spin and level up your golfer.

From first tee to five-star resort

Everything in Tee Town happens on a course you made.

Design every hole

Shape the land, cut fairways, dig bunkers, flood in water and plant trees. Then add clubhouses, snack stands, water parks and go-karts until it looks like nowhere else.

Golfers react to your design

Golfers play your course all day and tell you exactly what they think. Build holes they love, keep them happy, and the green fees roll in.

Then play every hole yourself

Pick a club, aim, shape a draw or fade and add spin. Every round earns XP to put into power, accuracy, spin and putting.

Share it and play everyone else's

Publish your course for other players, then explore theirs. Rate the courses you play and chase the course records.

Tournaments and the World Ranking

Enter solo events or hosted tournaments against real players with real purses. Win trophies for your case and climb the World Ranking.

Grow from a muni to a resort

Start with a few holes and work up to 18. Hit goals to raise your course level and unlock a golf inn, a helipad, a Grand Clubhouse and more.

More to build

Optional themed packs for when you want your course to stand out.

Find them in the Pro Shop in the game.
